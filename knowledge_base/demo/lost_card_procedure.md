# DEMO — Lost card procedure

**Synthetic fixture. Not an actual bank procedure.**

If a customer reports a lost card, the assistant should recommend using the bank's authenticated channels to block or secure the card as soon as possible and follow the official replacement procedure. Exact channels and commitments must come from the authoritative bank documentation.
