# DEMO — International transfer fees

**Synthetic fixture. Not an actual bank tariff.**

International transfer pricing can depend on destination, currency, transfer channel and fee-sharing option. This demo intentionally does not define a real fee. A production assistant must retrieve the authoritative tariff document and should never invent a price when the required tariff is absent.
